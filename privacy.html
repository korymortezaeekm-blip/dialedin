Dialed In – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy covers the Dialed In practice-exam app for Android (package com.dialedinprep.app), published by Midwest Solutions LLC ("we", "us").

Summary

Dialed In does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it never shows a permission prompt. It works without an internet connection and does not send your answers, progress or settings anywhere.

Android lists two kinds of standard access for the app: network access, which is declared by the software framework the app is built on, and vibration, which is used only for the optional haptic feedback you can turn off in Settings. The app does not use network access to transmit any information about you.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your study data in its private storage on your device: your answers, the review schedule, mock exam history, daily activity, any issue notes you write about a question, and your settings such as exam date, daily goal and theme. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it as described below. You can delete it at any time with "Reset progress" in the app's Settings, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include this app's saved data in your personal Google backup so it can be restored on a new device. That backup is operated by Google under your Google account and is not accessible to us.

Sharing that you start

"Export progress" and "Issue reports" in Settings open Android's share sheet with a copy of your study data or your issue notes. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app or a person to send it to, and we do not receive it unless you choose to email it to us.

Links to other websites

The app includes links to pages on the Project Management Institute website, such as the public exam outline. Tapping one opens it in your web browser. What that website collects is governed by its own privacy policy.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software and shares no data with third parties. The app is distributed through Google Play; Google's handling of your purchase, download and account information is governed by Google's own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Dialed In is a professional study tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.
